Focaccia

Cook: 22 min

Source: Smitten Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (520 g) flour
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp instant yeast
  • 2 cups water
  • 4 Tbsp olive oil
  • Flaky sea salt

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour (4 cups/520 g), salt (2 tsp), and yeast (1 tsp instant — important to use instant yeast, not active dry).
  2. Add the water (2 cups) — lukewarm for a 1–1.5 hour rise, or cold for an 8–10 hour rise — and mix until a loose, sticky dough forms. No kneading needed.
  3. Cover and let rise: at room temperature for the short rise, or in the fridge for the long rise.
  4. Heat the oven to 425°F. Pour 3 Tbsp of the olive oil onto a rimmed baking sheet and spread with your hands. Scoop the dough onto the sheet and roll it to coat in oil, then let it rest 20 min (60 min if it rose in the fridge).
  5. With oiled hands, stretch and press the dough into a rectangle filling the sheet. Drizzle with the remaining 1 Tbsp olive oil and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
  6. Bake 22 min (20–25 min), until lightly golden and puffy. Cool before slicing.

Tip: Freeze extras in a freezer bag the same day you bake — toast to thaw. Without store-bought preservatives, it won't stay fresh long at room temp.

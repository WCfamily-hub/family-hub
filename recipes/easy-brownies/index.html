Easy Brownies

Cook: 25–30 min

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/3 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/4 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Melt the butter (1/2 cup) in a saucepan on the stove (use a medium saucepan if you want this to be a one-pan recipe).
  3. Remove from heat and stir in the sugar (1 cup), then the eggs (2), then the vanilla (1 tsp).
  4. Add the cocoa powder (1/3 cup), flour (1/2 cup), and salt (1/4 tsp) and stir to combine.
  5. Pour into a greased 8" pan and bake 25–30 min (or ~340°F for 17 min in a toaster oven).

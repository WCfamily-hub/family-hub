Weg Family Baked Bao

Serves 16–24 buns | Cook: 22–25 min

Source: Woks of Life

Ingredients

Dough

  • 2/3 cup heavy cream, room temperature
  • 1 cup + 1 Tbsp milk, room temperature
  • 1 large egg, room temperature
  • 1/3 cup sugar
  • 4 cups (570 g) flour
  • 1 Tbsp active dry yeast
  • 1½ tsp salt

Filling

  • 1/2 onion
  • 1/3 head green cabbage
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 1 egg
  • 1/2 Tbsp sugar
  • 3 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 Tbsp hoisin sauce

Instructions

  1. Finely dice the onion (1/2) and grate the cabbage (1/3 head) on a mandoline's thinnest setting.
  2. In a large pan over medium heat, sauté the pork (1 lb), cabbage, onion, egg (1), sugar (1/2 Tbsp), soy sauce (3 Tbsp), and salt (1 tsp), stirring frequently, until cooked. Stir in the hoisin sauce (2 Tbsp) and let cool — this is the filling.
  3. In a stand mixer bowl, let the heavy cream (2/3 cup), milk (1 cup + 1 Tbsp), and egg (1 large) come to room temperature, stirring to break up the egg.
  4. Add the sugar (1/3 cup), flour (4 cups/570 g), yeast (1 Tbsp), and salt (1½ tsp). Knead on low with the dough hook for 15 min — the dough should stick to the bottom of the bowl but not the sides; add more flour 1 Tbsp at a time if needed.
  5. Shape into a ball, cover, and proof in a warm spot for 1h15–1h30 min, until doubled, then knead 5 min to deflate.
  6. On a floured surface, divide the dough into 16–24 pieces (~70 g each) and roll each into a 4-inch circle, thicker in the center than at the edges. Add a spoonful of filling, crimp tightly closed, and place seam-side down on parchment-lined baking sheets, 3 inches apart. Cover and let rise 1 hour.
  7. Heat the oven to 400°F with racks in the top and bottom thirds. Transfer the buns to the oven and immediately reduce the temperature to 350°F. Bake 22–25 min until golden brown. Cool before serving.

Tip: Keep your hands clean while filling — any grease on them makes the buns hard to seal shut.

Biscuits

Serves 15 small biscuits | Cook: 12–15 min

Source: Smitten Kitchen

Ingredients

  • 250 g (2 cups) flour, all purpose
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder
  • 1 Tbsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1.5 cups (355 mL) heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Mix together the flour (250 g/2 cups), baking powder (1 Tbsp), sugar (1 Tbsp), and salt (1/2 tsp).
  3. Add the heavy cream (1.5 cups) and mix into a dough — you might not need all of it, so try adding all but 1/4 cup first.
  4. Scoop into 2" rounds with an ice cream scoop (or press the dough 3/4" thick and use a round cutter) and place on the baking sheet.
  5. Bake 12–15 min, until the tops are golden. Or freeze the dough rounds and bake straight from the freezer with a few extra minutes (toaster oven: ~400°F for 12 min fresh, 370°F for 14 min frozen).

Tip: These freeze well, so next time try doubling the recipe — 500 g flour, 2 Tbsp baking powder, 2 Tbsp sugar, 1 tsp salt, 3 cups cream.

Baozi

Nainai's recipe | Makes ~16 buns | Total: ~2.5 hrs

Ingredients

  • Dough
  • 1 pkg active dry yeast
  • 4 cups flour
  • 1.5 cups water
  • Savory filling
  • 1 lb ground beef, pork, or turkey
  • Equal volume of a vegetable, finely chopped or shredded (raw butternut squash, cooked green beans, or raw cabbage)
  • 1/2 onion, finely diced
  • 1 egg
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 3 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 tsp salt
  • Optional: minced garlic or ginger
  • If using turkey: add 1 Tbsp oil
  • — OR Sweet filling —
  • 1 cup creamy peanut butter
  • 3 Tbsp sugar (or to taste)
  • 3 Tbsp hot water

Instructions

  1. Make the dough: Add the yeast (1 pkg) to 1/2 cup of warm water, let sit 5 min, then stir.
  2. Add the flour (4 cups) to a large mixing bowl. When the yeast is ready, add the yeast/water mixture and the remaining 1 cup of water and stir.
  3. Knead about 5 min, then cover the bowl with a towel and let sit for 10 min.
  4. Knead another 3–5 min until smooth, then cover again and let rise for 50–60 min.
  5. Make the filling: Thoroughly mix the ingredients for the sweet or savory filling in a bowl.
  6. Assemble and cook, for savory filling / bao shape: After the dough has risen, cut it into 4 equal parts.
  7. Take one part out at a time onto a lightly floured cutting board. Squeeze and roll the dough into a log (about 12" long), then cut into 1" pieces.
  8. Sprinkle flour over the dough pieces, then shape them into discs. Use a rolling pin to roll the discs out into flat, thin circles.
  9. Add a Tbsp of filling to the center of each circle, then pinch the edges closed. Let the shaped bao rest 10 min before cooking.
  10. To cook: heat a large pan over medium heat and add a thin layer of oil. Add a single layer of bao and let heat a few minutes. Add 1/2 cup water, cover, and turn down the heat until the water simmers. Cook 5–10 min, without opening the lid.
  11. Or, for sweet filling / roll shape: After the dough has risen, cut it into 4 equal parts.
  12. Take one part out at a time onto a lightly floured cutting board. Use a rolling pin to roll the dough into a large rectangle (about the size of a medium cutting board).
  13. Spread the filling over the rectangle, then roll up into a log and cut into 1" pieces. Let the pieces rest 10 min before cooking.
  14. To cook: heat a large pan over medium heat and add a thin layer of oil. Add a single layer of rolled dough pieces and let heat a few minutes. Add 1/2 cup water, cover, and turn down the heat until the water simmers. Cook 5–10 min, without opening the lid.

Tip: To reheat, place the bao or rolls in an unoiled pan, cover, and heat over low heat for about 5 min, or until warm.

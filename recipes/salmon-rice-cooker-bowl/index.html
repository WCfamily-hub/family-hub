Salmon Bowl in a Rice Cooker

Serves 1–2 | Cook: ~25 min (one rice cooker cycle)

Source: Kylie Sakaida, MS, RD (TikTok)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1 cup water
  • 1/2 cup chopped bok choy
  • 1/3 cup frozen shelled edamame
  • 6 oz salmon
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp oyster or hoisin sauce
  • 1 Tbsp sesame oil

Toppings

  • Diced cucumber
  • Avocado
  • Kewpie mayo
  • Sriracha
  • Green onion
  • Toasted seaweed
  • Optional: pickled ginger, seaweed salad

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice (1 cup) in the rice cooker bowl.
  2. Add the water (1 cup).
  3. Add the bok choy (1/2 cup) and edamame (1/3 cup).
  4. Place the salmon (6 oz) on top.
  5. Mix the soy sauce (2 Tbsp), oyster or hoisin sauce (1 Tbsp), and sesame oil (1 Tbsp) in a separate container, then pour over everything in the rice cooker bowl.
  6. Start the rice cooker and run a normal cycle.
  7. When done, mix everything together and top with the toppings of your choice.

Tip: This makes one generous bowl or two smaller ones — double the base ingredients (not the toppings) to feed more.

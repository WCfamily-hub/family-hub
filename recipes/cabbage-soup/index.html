Cabbage Soup

Serves 6–8 | Cook: 45 min

Source: New York Times

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 2 leeks or onions
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1/8 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 small head green cabbage
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper, plus more to taste
  • 2 quarts vegetable or chicken stock
  • 1 cup rice
  • 1–2 Parmesan rinds
  • 1 cup frozen mukimame (shelled edamame)
  • 1 lemon
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan, plus more for serving
  • 1 cup fresh dill or cilantro

Instructions

  1. Rinse, halve, and thinly slice the leeks or onions (2 medium); mince or finely grate the garlic (3 cloves); core and coarsely chop the cabbage (1 small head); rinse and chop the dill or cilantro (1 cup).
  2. Melt the butter (2 Tbsp) in a stockpot or large Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the leeks or onion and cook, stirring occasionally, until soft and lightly golden, 5–7 min.
  3. Stir in the garlic, red pepper flakes (1/8 tsp), cabbage, salt (1 1/2 tsp), and black pepper (1/2 tsp). Cook, stirring occasionally, until the cabbage wilts slightly and starts to caramelize, about 10 min.
  4. Stir in the stock (2 quarts) and rice (1 cup), then add the Parmesan rinds. Bring to a simmer and cook partly covered over medium-low heat until the cabbage and rice are very tender, 20–25 min. Add the mukimame with 5 min left.
  5. Discard the Parmesan rinds. Finely grate the lemon zest into the soup, then squeeze in the juice from half the lemon. Stir in the grated Parmesan (1/4 cup) and the dill or cilantro. Taste and add more salt and pepper if needed.
  6. Ladle into bowls and top with more grated Parmesan, a lemon wedge, and extra red pepper flakes if you'd like.

Tip: Save the Parmesan rind from your next block of cheese in the freezer specifically for soups like this one — it adds a lot of savory depth while it simmers.

Mug Cake

Serves 1 | Cook: 60 sec

Ingredients

  • 3 Tbsp sugar
  • 3 Tbsp flour
  • 1½ Tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2 Tbsp chocolate chips, plus extra for the top
  • 1½ Tbsp oil
  • 3 Tbsp milk
  • ¼ tsp vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. In a 10–12 oz microwave-safe mug, mix the sugar (3 Tbsp), flour (3 Tbsp), cocoa powder (1½ Tbsp), and salt (1 pinch) with a small fork or whisk until no lumps remain. Stir in the chocolate chips (2 Tbsp).
  2. Pour in the oil (1½ Tbsp), milk (3 Tbsp), and vanilla extract (¼ tsp). Stir gently until just combined and smooth, without overmixing.
  3. Sprinkle a few extra chocolate chips on top.
  4. Microwave on high for 60 sec. A few fudgy or slightly wet spots in the center are fine; if it looks completely liquid, microwave an additional 10 sec.
  5. Let rest 2–4 min to finish setting, then serve straight from the mug — with ice cream or whipped cream if you like.

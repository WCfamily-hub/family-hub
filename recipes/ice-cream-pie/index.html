Ice Cream Pie

Serves 8 | Freeze: 4 hr

Source: Family recipe

Ingredients

  • 1½ quarts vanilla ice cream
  • 1½ cups chocolate chip cookies
  • 1 prepared 9" graham cracker crust
  • ¾ cup chocolate sauce

Instructions

  1. Let the ice cream (1½ quarts) sit out until softened but still scoopable, about 10–15 min, and crush the chocolate chip cookies (1½ cups).
  2. Spread a third of the ice cream over the graham cracker crust, sprinkle with half the cookie crumbs, then drizzle with a third of the chocolate sauce (¾ cup).
  3. Spread another third of the ice cream on top, sprinkle with the remaining cookie crumbs, and drizzle with another third of the chocolate sauce.
  4. Spread the last of the ice cream on top and drizzle with the remaining chocolate sauce.
  5. Cover and freeze at least 4 hours, or until firm, before slicing.

Tip: Use our graham cracker pie crust recipe for the base.

Thanksgiving Recipes

Four go-to dishes for the Thanksgiving table

Turkey

Serves 10–14 | Roast: ~2–2.5 hr (plus 1–2 days to dry-brine)

Ingredients

  • 1 (12–16 lb) turkey
  • 3–4 Tbsp kosher salt
  • 4 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted
  • 8–10 medium onions (red or yellow), peeled and quartered
  • 1–2 Tbsp high-heat oil (sunflower or safflower)
  • Foil, for covering

Instructions

  1. Buying the turkey: estimate 1 to 1½ lbs per person. If frozen, defrost 2–3 days ahead in the fridge.
  2. 1–2 days before: put the turkey on a rack in a roasting pan, remove the giblets, and sprinkle kosher salt all over (about 1 Tbsp per 4 lb of bird), including some in the cavity. Cover loosely with plastic wrap and refrigerate 1–2 days.
  3. 1–2 hours before roasting: let the turkey come to room temperature (1–2 hr). Discard any juices that collected in the pan — no need to rinse off the salt.
  4. Peel the onions and cut into halves or quarters; toss with the oil.
  5. 3–4 hours before serving: heat the oven to 425°F with a rack on the lowest level. Stuff the turkey if desired, truss the legs with kitchen twine, and arrange the onions around it. Whisk 1 Tbsp of the melted butter with the maple syrup and brush this all over the turkey.
  6. Roast at 425°F for 25–30 min, then reduce the heat to 350°F and continue roasting until a thermometer in the thickest part of the breast reads 150–155°F — a 14–16 lb bird takes 2–2.5 hr total. Tent with foil once the skin is as dark as you want, removing it for the last 5–10 min so the skin re-crisps. Baste periodically and rotate the pan a couple of times for even cooking.
  7. Let the turkey rest, tented loosely with foil, 20–30 min before carving (carving takes 15–20 min). Use that time to reheat any sides that need the oven.
  8. Pour the collected juices over the carved turkey (save extra for gravy), finish with a sprinkle of salt and pepper, and arrange the onions all around.

Green Beans — Option 1 (with Mushroom Sauce & Crispy Onions)

Serves 6–8 | Bake: 15 min

Ingredients

  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 12 oz mushrooms, thinly sliced or coarsely chopped
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • 3/4 tsp table salt
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 Tbsp flour
  • 1½ cups vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 to 1.5 lbs green beans, trimmed
  • 1 cup crispy onions

Instructions

  1. Trim the green beans, chop the mushrooms, and mince the garlic.
  2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Boil the green beans 5 min, then drain and plunge into ice water. Drain again and set aside.
  3. Melt the butter in a 12-inch cast-iron skillet. Add the mushrooms, salt, and pepper, and sauté until they release their liquid, 3–5 min. Add the garlic and sauté 1 min more.
  4. Stir in the flour. Add the broth in 4–5 batches, stirring the whole time. Simmer 1 min, then add the cream. Bring to a simmer for 5–6 min, stirring frequently.
  5. Stir the cooked green beans into the mushroom sauce. Sprinkle the crispy onions over the top.
  6. Bake at 400°F for 15 min and serve at once.

Green Beans — Option 2 (Cacio e Pepe)

Serves 6 | Cook: ~10 min

Ingredients

  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 lbs green beans, stem ends trimmed
  • Salt and coarsely ground black pepper
  • 2 oz Pecorino Romano cheese, finely grated (1/2 cup)

Instructions

  1. Trim the ends off the green beans.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large, high-sided sauté pan with a lid (or a Dutch oven) over medium heat. Add the green beans and cook, tossing with tongs frequently, for 5 min.
  3. Add pepper, season with salt, and cook, stirring frequently, 1 min.
  4. Carefully pour ⅔ cup of water into the pan and cover immediately. Cook, tossing halfway through, until the beans are vibrant green and crisp-tender and the water has almost completely evaporated, about 4 min.
  5. Uncover, remove from the heat, and toss again. Transfer to a serving platter in an even layer and sprinkle the Pecorino Romano over the beans.

Garlicky Mashed Potato Cake

Serves 6–8 | Bake: ~1.5 hr total

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs russet potatoes (about 6 medium-large), scrubbed
  • 6 Tbsp butter
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • 1/2 cup sour cream, at room temperature

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Scrub the potatoes.
  2. Bake the potatoes directly on the oven rack for 1 hr to 1 hr 15 min. Remove and let sit at room temperature just until cool enough to handle (they should still be hot).
  3. Peel and smash the garlic.
  4. Heat a 10-inch nonstick, oven-safe skillet over medium heat. Add the butter and swirl until melted and foamy, then add the garlic and cook, stirring occasionally, until both are golden brown, about 3 min. Remove from the heat and set aside.
  5. Cut the still-hot potatoes in half and scoop the flesh onto a cutting board. Chop into pieces no larger than a walnut half.
  6. Add the potato flesh to the skillet. Season generously with salt, pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg. Add the sour cream and gently fold until evenly coated, then flatten into a single layer. Cook over medium-high heat 5–8 min.
  7. Shake the skillet to loosen, then transfer to the oven and bake 30–35 min. Let sit 10 min, then carefully invert the potato cake onto a serving platter. (Can be made ahead: leave in the skillet at room temperature, then reheat at 425°F for 10–15 min before serving.)

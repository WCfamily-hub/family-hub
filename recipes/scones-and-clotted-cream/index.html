Scones and Clotted Cream

Yield: 12 servings | Prep: 20 min | Cook: ~10 hr for cream + 8 min bake for scones

Ingredients

  • 4 cups (1 quart) heavy cream
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 4 Tbsp unsalted butter, diced
  • 1 large egg, beaten
  • 5 Tbsp milk
  • 1 large egg, beaten (for glazing)

Instructions

  1. Heat the cream in a double boiler (or a heatproof bowl over simmering water) to 175°F, then raise to 180–200°F and maintain that temperature for 45 min–1 hr, until a cracked, yellow "skin" forms.
  2. Transfer the bowl to an ice-water bath to cool quickly, then cover and chill in the fridge overnight (about 12 hr). Carefully skim off the thickened clotted cream and set aside.
  3. Preheat the oven to 425°F and line a baking sheet with butter or parchment.
  4. Combine the flour, baking powder, and salt; work in the butter until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  5. Make a well and add the beaten egg and milk, mixing to form a soft dough.
  6. Turn the dough onto a floured surface, knead briefly until it comes together, then roll to about 1-inch thickness and cut into rounds.
  7. Place the rounds on the sheet, brush with the beaten egg, and bake until golden, about 8 min.

Breakfast Burritos

Makes 12 burritos | Freezer-friendly

Ingredients

  • 12 frozen hash brown patties
  • 24 slices fully cooked bacon
  • 18 large eggs
  • ⅓ cup sour cream
  • 1½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¾ tsp black pepper
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 12 flour tortillas, 10-inch
  • 12 oz sharp cheddar, shredded
  • Also: heavy-duty foil, parchment paper, 2-gallon freezer bags

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Bake the hash brown patties (12) for 22–28 min, flipping once, until deep brown and crisp. Cool on a rack, then cut each patty into 4 or 5 strips.
  2. Lay the bacon (24 slices) in a single layer in a cold dry skillet. Cook over medium heat for 3–4 min, turning, until it stiffens. Drain and blot hard on paper towels, then chop into ½-inch pieces.
  3. Whisk the eggs (18) with the sour cream (⅓ cup), salt (1½ tsp), and pepper (¾ tsp).
  4. Melt the butter (3 Tbsp) in a nonstick skillet over medium-low. Add the eggs and push them slowly for 6–8 min, pulling the pan off the heat while still glossy and slightly wet. They finish cooking twice more.
  5. Spread the potatoes, bacon, and eggs in thin layers on sheet pans and cool to room temperature, 20–30 min, or 15 min in the fridge. Nothing should feel warm.
  6. Stack the tortillas (12) under a damp towel and microwave 30–45 sec, until pliable.
  7. Shred the cheddar (12 oz). Across the lower third of each tortilla, layer a small handful of cheese, then eggs, bacon, potato strips, and a last pinch of cheese — about ¾ cup filling total.
  8. Fold the bottom edge up over the filling, pull it back toward you to compress, fold both sides in, and roll. Set seam side down.
  9. Wrap each burrito in parchment, then foil. Write the date on the foil.
  10. Freeze on a sheet pan for 1 hour, then pack into gallon freezer bags and press out the air.

Reheating from frozen (toaster oven): Peel off the parchment, leave the foil on. Bake at 350°F for 30–35 min. Unwrap completely, return it seam side down, and bake 8–12 min more, until the tortilla blisters. On convection, use 325°F and start checking 5 min early. The center should read 165°F on an instant-read thermometer — if low, bake unwrapped 5 min at a time. Reheating thawed (toaster oven): after thawing overnight in the fridge, bake at 350°F, foil on for 12 min, then foil off for 8–10 min.

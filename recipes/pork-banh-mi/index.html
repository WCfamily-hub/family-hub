Pork Banh Mi

Serves 4 | Marinate: 10–20 min | Cook: ~15 min

Source: Food52

Ingredients

  • 1–1.5 lbs pork tenderloin
  • 2 Tbsp vegetable oil
  • Sliced jalapeño peppers
  • Cilantro
  • Mayonnaise
  • 1 loaf sweet French baguette
  • Pickled Carrots and Radishes:
  • 2–3 carrots, thinly sliced
  • 3–4 radishes, thinly sliced
  • 1 Tbsp lime juice
  • 1 Tbsp water or fish sauce
  • 1/2 Tbsp sugar
  • Sauce:
  • 1 slice ginger, minced
  • 1 green onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 3 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 Tbsp maple syrup
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1/2 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Mix the sauce (ginger, green onion, brown sugar, fish sauce, maple syrup, soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, and pepper).
  2. Cut the pork tenderloin across the grain into 1/2-inch pieces. Flatten each piece to an even 1/4 inch between two pieces of saran wrap using a meat pounder or rolling pin. Use a few Tbsp of the sauce to marinate the meat for 10–20 min (or overnight in the refrigerator).
  3. Pickle the carrots and radishes: mix in a bowl with the lime juice, water or fish sauce, and sugar. Let sit 20 min.
  4. Heat a grill or grill pan to high and heat the oil. Sear the first side of the meat until very dark brown, then flip and sear the second side. If the meat isn't thin enough to be done at this point, transfer to a 350°F oven and finish baking for 5–10 min.
  5. Assemble sandwiches or a rice bowl!

Tip: Works as a Banh Mi sandwich or as a rice bowl — the sauce is great on anything. Cucumber Radish Salad can be used instead of the pickled carrots and radishes.

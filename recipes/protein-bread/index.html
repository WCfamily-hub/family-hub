Protein Bread

Cook: 40–45 min

Source: NYTimes

Ingredients

  • 1½ tsp active dry yeast
  • 1/3 cup (75 g) lukewarm water
  • 1 cup (250 g) egg whites, 7–8 large
  • 1 cup (255 g) cottage cheese
  • 4⅔ cups (582 g) bread flour, plus more for dusting
  • 1 Tbsp kosher salt, or 1½ tsp fine salt

Instructions

  1. Combine the yeast (1½ tsp) and lukewarm water (1/3 cup/75 g) in a large bowl and let sit 5–10 min until foamy.
  2. In a blender or food processor, blend the egg whites (1 cup/250 g) and cottage cheese (1 cup/255 g) until smooth. Add to the yeast mixture and whisk to combine.
  3. Whisk the bread flour (4⅔ cups/582 g) in its container to aerate, then add it along with the salt (1 Tbsp) to the wet ingredients. Stir to form a shaggy, sticky dough.
  4. On a floured surface, knead the dough 5–6 min until smooth — use a bench scraper to lift sticky dough as needed, or oil your hands. A stand mixer or bread machine also works.
  5. Shape into a ball, return to the bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm spot 1 hour until almost doubled. Punch down, roll into a log, pinch the seam closed, and place seam-side down in a parchment-lined 9×5" loaf pan. Let rise 20–25 min until visibly puffed.
  6. Heat the oven to 400°F. Score the top with a sharp knife and bake 40–45 min until the crust sounds hollow when tapped, covering loosely with foil once the top browns. Cool completely before slicing.
